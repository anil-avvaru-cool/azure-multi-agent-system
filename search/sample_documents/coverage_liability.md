# Liability Coverage

Redwood Auto P&C liability coverage pays for bodily injury and property damage
you cause to others in a covered accident, up to the limits shown on your
declarations page. It does not cover damage to your own vehicle.

Bodily injury liability pays for the other party's medical expenses, lost
wages, and legal defense costs if you are sued. Property damage liability
pays to repair or replace the other party's vehicle or property.

Liability coverage is required in most states to legally operate a vehicle.
Minimum limits vary by state; Redwood recommends limits above the state
minimum for meaningful financial protection.
