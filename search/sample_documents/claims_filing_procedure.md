# Claims Filing Procedure

To file a claim, contact Redwood's FNOL (First Notice of Loss) line or submit
through the customer portal as soon as possible after the incident. Have
your policy number, the date/time/location of the incident, and a
description of what happened ready before you start.

After intake, a claims adjuster reviews the claim, coverage applicable under
your policy is confirmed, and — for damage claims — a repair estimate is
scheduled. Straight-through processing may apply for eligible low-complexity
claims; higher-complexity or higher-value claims are routed to an adjuster
for manual review. You will receive a claim number to track status.
