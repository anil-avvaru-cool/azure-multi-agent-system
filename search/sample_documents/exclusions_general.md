# General Policy Exclusions

Redwood Auto P&C policies do not cover: intentional damage caused by the
policyholder, damage occurring while the vehicle is used for commercial
ridesharing or delivery without the applicable endorsement, racing or other
speed-contest use, damage from nuclear hazard or war, and normal wear, tear,
or mechanical breakdown.

Coverage is also excluded for driving under the influence of alcohol or
drugs, and for use by a driver excluded by name in the policy declarations.
An exclusion removes coverage entirely for the excluded scenario — it is not
subject to the deductible.
