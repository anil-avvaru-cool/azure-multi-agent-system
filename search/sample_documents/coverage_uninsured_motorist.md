# Uninsured and Underinsured Motorist Coverage

Uninsured motorist (UM) coverage pays for your bodily injury and, in some
states, property damage when the at-fault driver has no liability insurance.
Underinsured motorist (UIM) coverage pays the difference when the at-fault
driver's liability limits are insufficient to cover your losses.

This coverage does not depend on the other driver being identified or
located; hit-and-run accidents are covered under UM in most states. UM/UIM
limits cannot exceed your own liability limits.
